Use case 01 · Verification
iv

From “I found a name” to “I have evidence.”

Help the customer move from a hunch to a defensible decision in four moves.

i

Start with the entity

Company name, country, product, HS code, and buyer or supplier role.

ii

Cross‑check trade signals

Look for shipment patterns, trade lanes, counterparties, markets and consistency.

iii

Validate risk context

Screen sanctions, company context, and market plausibility.

iv

Decide what to do next

Proceed, ask more questions, request documents, or exclude.

Affiliate angle

“Before you send money, sign a contract or schedule a meeting, use ADAMftd to verify whether the counterparty is commercially plausible.”

ADAMftd · Affiliate Sales Playbook 10 / 12
Safe language for affiliates
xi

Protect credibility: be specific, not exaggerated.

Two columns. Use the left. Avoid the right.

+Say this

  • AI-native trade intelligence grounded in source data.
  • Historical trade and shipment data, with reporting delays.
  • Coverage varies by country, commodity, source and availability.
  • Use ADAMftd to support analysis, verification and discovery.

−Avoid this

  • Implying real-time shipment tracking.
  • Promising guaranteed buyers or sales outcomes.
  • Using UN, WTO or International Trade Centre logos or language.
  • Quoting unsupported savings or “value created” figures.
