Qualify the prospect in the first 60 seconds This script is for trading companies: importers, exporters, distributors, manufacturers, trading houses, retailers. They buy or sell physical goods. If the prospect is an institutional consultant, commercial lawyer, trade finance provider, insurance underwriter or ratings agency, use the Institutional Demo Script instead. The institutional flow leads with verification and market reports, not with trade data discovery.

How to use this script

This is a 10-12 minute live demo flow. Open the platform alongside the prospect at adamftd.com. Follow the order. Read the talk tracks word-for-word the first three times you do it, then make them yours.

  • Total runtime: 10-12 minutes plus questions.
  • The single biggest WOW is the HS Disambiguator (Module 3) and the Trade History Pivot (Module 5).
  • Modules 4 (Views) and 7 (Docs) are optional add-ons. Drop them if the prospect is engaged on something else.
  • In Module 5 you must know whether the prospect is BUYER or SELLER. Ask at the end of Module 4.
  • Each module ends with the question in yellow. That is how you find their pain.

The Opening (60 seconds)

First 60 seconds of every demo. Frame the magnet before you show anything.

The ADAM Assistant dashboard (the login landing screen).
URL: adamftd.com/dashboard. You will see the ADAM chat header, six quick-action cards (Top importers, Importing countries, Market price, Market sentiment, Retail snapshot, Consumer preferences), and a Trade Data entry in the left sidebar under TOOLS.

Tell me what you trade. A product, a market, an industry. In the next ten minutes I will show you everything we know about the people who already trade that thing globally. Real shipments. Real buyer names. Real supplier names. Real prices. Real volumes. Real dates. Not estimates. Not industry reports. Bills of lading. The actual transactions. Then I will show you what you do with that data. Calculate landed cost on a specific corridor. Generate the shipping documents. The whole thing lives in one platform.

Market Search (60 seconds)

When to use
Immediately after the opening. Goal: trigger the AI HS lookup.
Screen to open
Trade Data tool from sidebar.
Real page title: Customs Declarations & Shipping Data. URL: adamftd.com/trade-data. You will see Export Country, Import Country, HS Code or Product Name field, and Search button. Leave the countries on "Any Country" for the opener.
Talk track
I am going to type the product the prospect just named. No HS code knowledge required, the platform handles that. I hit search. Watch what happens next, because this is where most platforms fall flat and ours does not.
Action
Type the product (e.g. coffee) in the HS Code or Product Name field and click Search. The "AI Lookup" tag will appear.

HS Disambiguator (90 seconds)

WOW
When to use
Triggers automatically after Module 2 search. This is one of two main WOW beats.
Screen to open
The "Select the best HS code for [product]" panel appears.
Each row shows: HS 6-digit code, live buyer count, live supplier count, the official product description, and real example brands. The top recommended row carries a green Recommended badge.
Talk track
You typed coffee. The platform broke that into the real HS categories used in customs filings. Roasted coffee beans: 4,575 buyers, 2,510 suppliers globally. Unroasted green coffee beans: 11,289 buyers, 5,174 suppliers. Instant coffee: 6,000 buyers, 3,363 suppliers. These are real companies filing real customs paperwork. Even better, the platform shows you what brand names sit inside each category. Starbucks Whole Bean. Lavazza Espresso. Nespresso. Nestle. So you do not need to know HS codes. You pick the one that matches what you sell or buy and we do the rest.
Action
Click the Recommended row. The platform resolves it with a High Confidence badge. The search refines automatically.
Close the module
When you classify a product today for customs or quotes, who does that and how often does it slow you down?

12 Views on the Same Data (90 seconds)

WOW
When to use
Right after the HS code resolves. Show the breadth before you drill into one company.
Screen to open
The "VIEW RESULTS AS" panel below the search.
12 buttons: Buyer Countries, Supplier Countries, Buyers, Suppliers, Bills of Lading, Unit Price (Buyers), Unit Price (Suppliers), Market Avg Price, Market Analytics, Delivery Ports, Origin Ports, Tariffs. Plus a unit toggle: USD / B/L / WEIGHT / QTY. Plus three layout icons (treemap, two-pane, table) and Export button.
Talk track
One search, twelve angles. Want to see which countries import the most? Click. Which buyers? Click. Which bills of lading were filed last quarter? Click. What was the average unit price paid by buyers in Mexico versus buyers in Japan? Two clicks. Which port handles the most volume into your country? One click. The platform is built so you do not need a data analyst. You just click the angle you need.
Action
Click Buyers. Switch to the table view icon (third icon, far right). Toast confirms "Found [N] records." Real numbers appear: top buyer Nestle Argentina S.a $28.03M, Nespresso Mexico $22.26M, WAL-MART $20.4M.
There is a banner: "US Dollar values are not recorded for the majority of US imports and exports." If the prospect is US-heavy, toggle to B/L or WEIGHT so the numbers feel right.
Close the module
Quick check before I drill in. Are you on the buy side or the sell side of this product?

Trade History Pivot (2 minutes)

WOW
When to use
After Module 4. This is THE pivot. You now know if the prospect is BUYER or SELLER. Use the matching branch below.
Screen to open
Click a company name in the Buyers table. You land on the company profile page.
Tabs across the top: Overview, Contacts, Web & Media, Reviews, Trade Analytics, HS Codes, Customers (who they sell to), Suppliers (who they buy from), Bills of Lading. Plus three buttons top right: PDF Report, Bookmark, Enrich Company. The Overview shows Total Buyers, Total Suppliers, Jurisdiction, Status (Active/Inactive), and Sanctions (Clear / Flagged).
Talk track
If prospect is a BUYER
You buy this product. Here is one of the suppliers we just saw. We are now on the company profile. Sanctions status: Clear. Jurisdiction: visible. Click Customers tab. Now you see every buyer this supplier sells to, the trade value, the bills of lading count, the weight, the share of their total business. You can see if they give your competitor a better deal. You can see if they ship to anyone on a watchlist you care about. Click Suppliers tab if you want to see who they source from. Click Bills of Lading for every individual shipment. Before you renegotiate, before you onboard them, you walk in knowing everything.
If prospect is a SELLER
You sell this product. Here is one of the buyers we just saw. We are now on the company profile. Sanctions status: Clear. Active company. Click Suppliers tab. Now you see every supplier they currently buy from. Trade value, bills of lading count, weight, quantity, share. Look at this. Their number one supplier owns 50.4 percent of their volume. The number two supplier is 48.3 percent. There is a 1.2 percent third player. You walk into your pitch knowing exactly what you are competing against, what they pay, and where the gap is.
Close the module
Imagine you had this view on every counterparty you negotiate with this year. What would it change?
6

Duty and Landed Cost (90 seconds)

When to use
After the pivot. Frame as "you found the deal, now what does it actually cost to land?"
Screen to open
Sidebar: HS, Tariffs & Landed Costs.
Real page title: Duty & Tariff Calculator. URL: adamftd.com/calculator. Three numbered fields: Origin Country (From), Destination Country (To), HS Code or Product Description. There is also a History tab and a Transparency link.
Talk track
Same product. Pick origin. Pick destination. The platform asks "do you want full landed cost?" If yes, you add quantity and sale price and we model duties, fees, VAT, and import taxes. If you just want the duty picture, click Skip. Either way you get: the MFN duty rate, the WTO bound rate, any preferential trade agreement, additional fees like Merchandise Processing and Harbor Maintenance, state and local sales tax range, plus any political surcharges with their current legal status. For example, on coffee Brazil to USA, MFN is zero percent, but there is a ten percent Section 122 surcharge whose legal status is currently challenged. That kind of footnote is the difference between a confident quote and a missed margin.
Bonus
The same screen lists Regulatory Requirements (FDA, USDA APHIS for coffee), and a Required Documents badge. There is also an Export Certificate button and a Simplified / Detailed toggle.
Close the module
When you quote a deal today, how confident are you in the landed cost number?

Bridges between modules

One-liners to flow from one module into the next.

Search to HS Disambiguator: "Watch the next thing. You did not have to know any HS codes."
HS to Views: "Now that we have the right code, look at the angles we can slice this from."
Views to Pivot: "That is the market view. Now let me drill into one company so you can see what we know about them specifically."
Pivot to Tariffs: "You have the counterparty. Next question, what does it actually cost to move the goods?"
Tariffs to Docs: "You have the data and the cost. Last piece, how do you turn it into a transaction?"
Any module to skip: "I will jump ahead because you mentioned X earlier and that is where this gets useful for you specifically."
Wildcard modules (drop in only when relevant)

ADAM Assistant chat: The dashboard chat lets the prospect type natural language like "top coffee importers in Mexico last year" and the platform answers conversationally. Use this if the prospect is non-technical or you want to show off the AI side.

Hormuz Impact: A specialized 4-step wizard modeling oil shock scenarios across 5,000+ HS-6 product codes. Only relevant for energy traders, Middle East-exposed prospects, or commodity desks. Skip otherwise.

Other sidebar tools to mention (do not click in 10 minutes): Sanctions, Tenders, Vessels & Ports, Trade Companies, Company Lookup, Countries, Market Reports, Market Analysis, Enrichment, Search History.

Closing the call

After the last module, do not summarise. Ask one of these.

  • Of the seven things you saw, which one would change your work tomorrow?
  • If we gave your team a free seven-day trial starting Monday, what counterparty would you want to look up first?
  • Who else on your team should see this before you make a decision?

Then book the follow-up before you hang up. Calendar open on screen, propose two slots, close.

Different prospect, different demo If the prospect is a consultant, commercial lawyer, trade finance provider, insurance underwriter or ratings agency, do not use this script. Their value driver is not finding deals. It is verifying real trading activity (Sanctions: Clear, Active status, Bills of Lading evidence), doing market analysis and sentiment (Market Reports, Market Analysis modules in the sidebar), and pulling structured intelligence reports. Switch to the Institutional Demo Script (separate page, in production).
